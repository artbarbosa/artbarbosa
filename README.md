# Arthur Barbosa

**Mobile Software Engineer** · Android and iOS · Kotlin, Swift, Flutter and React Native · .NET back end

I build apps that move money, and I work on what goes wrong in them: the
response that gets lost, the process the OS kills mid-flight, the device with
no network.

More than six years shipping to production across fintech, streaming, travel
and field operations. Two and a half of them at Celcoin, on regulated payment
flows: Pix, POS, boletos, statements. I am currently a full-stack engineer
at Juvo, across mobile (Flutter and native) and C#/.NET.

### What I do best

- **Android and iOS apps, native and cross-platform**: Kotlin, Swift, Flutter
  and React Native; domain-based modular architecture, offline-first, testing
- **Native Swift and Kotlin modules** through platform channels: biometrics,
  cryptography, camera, POS peripherals
- **Correctness under partial failure**: idempotency, outbound queues,
  reconciliation
- **End to end when the product needs it**: back end in .NET (ASP.NET Core),
  Node.js and PHP, and React/TypeScript on the web
- **Production support for financial flows**: logs, incident analysis, fixing
  the flows people depend on

### Selected work
